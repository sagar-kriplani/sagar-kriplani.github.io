CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION01

BROOKS AUTOMATION · RESEARCH INTERNSHIP · 2023

Less complexity.
Better process air.

Redesigning the Process Air Unit for an EUV pod cleaner, from thermodynamic calculations to a more compact mechanical layout.

ThermodynamicsMechanical layoutComponent selection
Explore case study ↗

The challenge

Reassess an oversized clean-dry-air heating system, reduce pressure losses, and simplify the assembly within the available machine space.

My contribution

Analysed air requirements and pressure losses, compared heater concepts, selected components, and developed the mechanical design and documentation.

Outcome & context

The July 2023 project report records about 21% lower PAU cost, pressure drop reduced from approximately 500 to 50 mbar, and a heater resized from 48 to 18 kW. These are project design comparisons; capacity reduction does not establish operating energy savings.

Source: research internship report, abstract and conclusion (p. 53).

CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION03

EAH JENA / BROOKS AUTOMATION · MASTER’S THESIS · 2025

Test the idea.
Understand the trade-offs.

Comparing radiant heaters and IR LEDs for polymer heating inside FOUP cleaner vacuum chambers.

Experimental designFTIR / UV-VisThermal measurement
Explore case study ↗

The challenge

Understand how different FOUP polymers respond to radiant heaters and LEDs, and evaluate the engineering trade-offs of a potential alternative heating method.

My contribution

Investigated material absorption, selected LEDs, developed experimental arrangements, and compared heating behaviour and temperature distribution.

Outcome & context

The thesis found that LEDs offered more even surface heating in the tested arrangement, while conventional radiant heaters delivered better overall heating capacity and broader material compatibility. LED heat dissipation and temperature measurement remained limitations. This was a comparative investigation, not a production-ready LED replacement.

Source: submitted thesis, 27 February 2025; discussion and conclusion (pp. 73–76).
