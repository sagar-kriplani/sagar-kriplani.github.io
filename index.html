SOURCE REPORT EXCERPT03

EAH JENA / BROOKS AUTOMATION · MASTER’S THESIS · 2025

Test the idea.
Understand the trade-offs.

Comparing radiant heaters and IR LEDs for polymer heating inside FOUP cleaner vacuum chambers.

Experimental designFTIR / UV-VisThermal measurement
Explore case study ↗

The challenge

Understand how different FOUP polymers respond to radiant heaters and LEDs, and evaluate the engineering trade-offs of a potential alternative heating method.

My contribution

Investigated material absorption, selected LEDs, developed experimental arrangements, and compared heating behaviour and temperature distribution.

Outcome & context

The thesis found that LEDs offered more even surface heating in the tested arrangement, while conventional radiant heaters delivered better overall heating capacity and broader material compatibility. LED heat dissipation and temperature measurement remained limitations. This was a comparative investigation, not a production-ready LED replacement.

Source: submitted thesis, 27 February 2025; discussion and conclusion (pp. 73–76).

The challenge

Study temperature evolution and frost-layer behaviour on cold surfaces relevant to refrigeration and cryogenic heat transfer.

My contribution

Contributed as one of four named students to the numerical-model project. The report covers an energy equation, a finite-difference scheme, boundary conditions and transient MATLAB results. Individual task ownership is not specified in the report.

Outcome & context
